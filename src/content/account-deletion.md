# Comet Wallet — Account & Data Deletion

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Comet Wallet account and the personal data associated with it, in line with Google Play's User Data policy. It applies whether you signed up on Android, iOS, or the web.

## 1. HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

You can request deletion of your Comet Wallet account in either of the following ways:

**Option A — In the app**

1. Open the Comet Wallet app and log in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**.
3. Confirm your identity (password/PIN or biometric prompt) and confirm the deletion request.

**Option B — By email**

Send a deletion request to **info@cometagri.com** from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Please include:

- the phone number or email address registered to your Comet Wallet account; and
- your full name, as it appears on the account.

We may ask you to verify your identity before actioning the request, to ensure your account isn't deleted by someone impersonating you.

## 2. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR DATA

**2.1** Once we confirm your identity and process your request, your account is closed and you will no longer be able to log in or use the Comet Wallet app with those credentials.

**2.2** The following data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised:

- profile information (name, email, phone number, profile photo)
- saved contacts and beneficiaries picked from your address book
- device tokens, push-notification identifiers, and saved preferences
- support tickets and in-app chat history

**2.3** Some information cannot be deleted immediately and is retained for the periods below, as permitted or required by applicable law:

| Data | Retention period | Why we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| KYC/identity verification records (ID documents, selfie/liveness checks, proof of address) | Up to **8 years** after account closure | Required under Kenya's Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing obligations and the Data Protection Act, No. 24 of 2019 |
| Transaction history (transfers, deposits, withdrawals, bill payments, wallet balances) | Up to **8 years** after account closure | Financial recordkeeping and regulatory reporting obligations |
| Records relevant to an open dispute, claim, or fraud investigation | Until the matter is resolved | Legal claims and fraud prevention |

**2.4** Retained data is restricted to what is necessary for these purposes, is not used for marketing, and is securely deleted or anonymised once the applicable retention period expires.

## 3. TIMEFRAME

We aim to complete deletion requests, or explain any part we can't fulfil, within **30 days** of confirming your identity. If we need more time due to the complexity of your request, we will let you know.

## 4. QUESTIONS

For questions about this process or your data, contact us at **info@cometagri.com** or our Data Protection Officer at **compliance@mam-laka.com**. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for full detail on how we handle your personal information.
