# Comet Wallet — Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 15, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Mamlaka Hub and Spoke Limited, operating as **Comet Wallet** ("we," "us," or "our"), uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and web-based portions of the Comet Wallet Platform, and the choices available to you. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Use](/terms).

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, SDKs, and mobile device identifiers used within the Comet Wallet mobile app. We use these technologies to operate our services, keep your account secure, and understand how our Platform is used.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential cookies** — necessary for the operation of the website, including to authenticate users, prevent fraudulent use of accounts, and enable core features. These cannot be switched off.
- **Functional cookies** — allow us to recognise and count visitors and see how they move around the site, helping us improve how the Platform works.
- **Analytic cookies** — recognise you when you return to the Platform, enabling us to personalise content and remember your preferences.
- **Tracking cookies** — record your visit, the pages you've viewed, and the links you've followed, so we can make the Platform and its content more relevant to your interests. We may share this information with third parties for this purpose, only where you have consented.

## 3. Cookies vs. In-App Technologies

The Comet Wallet mobile app does not use browser cookies. Instead, similar functions — analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications — are performed through mobile SDKs (including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) and device identifiers. These are described in detail, along with the specific device permissions the app requests, in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where required by law, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings. If you choose to refuse, disable, or delete cookies, some Platform functionality, services, or features may no longer be available or function properly.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use, or for legal or regulatory reasons. Material changes will be notified through the Platform or by other appropriate means.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at **info@cometagri.com**.
