## 5. PERMISSIONS THE COMET WALLET APP REQUESTS AND WHY

The Comet Wallet mobile app requests the following device permissions. We only use each permission for the purpose described below, and most are optional — you can decline them and continue using the core wallet features.

| Permission | Why we request it |
|---|---|
| **Internet / network access** | Required for the app to connect to our servers and process any wallet, payment, or account activity. |
| **Camera** | Used to scan QR codes for payments and merchant checkout, and to capture your ID document and a selfie/liveness video during identity verification (KYC), processed through our identity verification provider. |
| **Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face unlock)** | Optional. Lets you unlock the app or approve transactions using your device's built-in biometric sensor. Your biometric data is captured, matched, and stored entirely on your device by its operating system — it is never transmitted to or stored on our servers. |
| **Contacts** | Optional. Lets you pick a contact from your address book when sending money, so you don't have to retype a phone number or email. We only read and use the specific contact you select for that transaction; we do not upload or store your full address book on our servers. |
| **Notifications** | Used to send transaction confirmations, security alerts, and (where you opt in) promotional or product updates. |
| **Vibrate** | Used for in-app haptic feedback (for example, on transaction confirmation). No data is collected. |
| **Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)** | Used through Firebase Analytics for aggregated app-usage analytics, install/campaign attribution, and fraud and abuse detection. It is not used to build advertising profiles for third-party ad networks. |
| **Device identifiers** | A persistent device identifier is used for account security, device management (so you can view and revoke devices logged into your account), and fraud prevention. |
